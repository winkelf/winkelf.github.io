      Which process happens

      Every click picks exactly one hard process. Each process i has a fixed weight wi, one value per rate mode. Processes that cannot happen in this event get zero weight, and the rest share the probability in proportion to their weights.

      P(i)=wiΘi∑jwjΘj

      Θi = 1 if every force that process i needs is switched on and the two protons contain a parton pair it can start from , carrying enough energy for it, x₁x₂s ≥ m² (m is the mass it makes, or its √ŝ threshold); otherwise 0. Lower the collision energy and the heavy processes drop out.

      The weights

      LHC-like weights are rough cross-sections in picobarns at √s = 13.6 TeV. Several depend on cuts that are not fixed here, so read them as orders of magnitude. The probabilities assume all three forces are on.

      Sampling the parton momenta

      Each proton gets three valence quarks (u u d), four to six gluons, and sea quark–antiquark pairs (u ū and d d̄ always, s s̄ in 60% of protons). Every parton draws a raw momentum fraction from a Beta distribution, a simple stand-in for a real parton distribution function:

      x~k∼f(x)∝xα−1(1−x)β−1

      Defaults: valence α = 0.75, β = 3.6 · gluon α = 0.5, β = 4.5 · sea α = 0.3, β = 9. Each draw uses inverse-CDF sampling: x̃ = F⁻¹(u) with u uniform in [0, 1] and F tabulated on a 2048-point grid.

      The raw fractions are then rescaled so the partons carry exactly the proton's momentum, the momentum sum rule:

      xk=x~k∑mx~m⇒∑kxk=1

      From partons to the hard process

      The two colliding partons fix the energy available for the hard interaction and how the final state is boosted along the beam:

      s^=x1x2s,y0=12lnx1x2

      Jets, tops and the exchange processes use the most energetic pair of partons that can start them:

      (i,j)=arg max(i,j)allowedxixj

      The Z, W, Higgs, Drell–Yan and diphoton processes instead fix the mass m of the produced system first. It comes from a Breit–Wigner for the Z and W, is fixed at 125 GeV for the Higgs, and falls steeply for the Drell–Yan and diphoton continua. Two allowed partons are then given exactly the momentum fractions needed:

      x1,2=mse±y,dσdm∝m−3(continua)

      y is uniform in [−1.6, 1.6]. Outgoing particles are emitted back to back in the partonic rest frame and then boosted by y₀ along the beam.

      Tune the parton distributions

      Inside a proton, QCD decides how the momentum is shared between quarks and gluons. Move the sliders to reshape each Beta distribution or change how many partons a proton holds. Every new collision in the Collider tab uses these settings.
